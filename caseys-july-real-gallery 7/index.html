(c) Our story

Rooted here since '94

Casey's opened on July 3rd, 1994 — a summer night that turned into a 32-year neighborhood tradition. We've watched the brick stores come and go and never once changed our window. The emerald walls were repainted once. The brass was never.

We're not a destination. We're the place you stop on the way home and end up staying through the third period.
